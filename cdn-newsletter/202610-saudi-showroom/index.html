Inside CDN LIGHT’s Saudi Arabia Showroom

Dear partners and friends,

Our Saudi Arabia showroom brings together CDN, BACH and Mayalit, offering a closer look at our lighting products and the expertise behind our brands.

Three brands, distinct areas of expertise

CDN offers indoor and outdoor lighting for commercial, residential and architectural projects, with solutions extending from interior spaces to building façades and landscapes.

BACH specializes in high-end hospitality lighting, with architectural lighting solutions that help shape the atmosphere and guest experience of hotels and resorts.

Mayalit focuses on museum and gallery lighting, addressing the precise needs of collection displays, from individual objects in showcases to artworks and exhibition spaces. Its remote-controlled spotlights allow users to adjust aiming and zoom remotely, making it easier to redirect the light and change the beam angle as displays evolve.

Experience the products and their light

The showroom combines product displays with illuminated settings. Visitors can examine luminaires up close, compare beam patterns and see how light interacts with walls, textures and planting.

Indoor architectural lighting and outdoor landscape displays provide a practical starting point for conversations about product selection and project requirements.

Outdoor lighting in context

Bollards and landscape luminaires are presented among planting, gravel and textured surfaces, offering a closer look at product scale, placement and lighting effects.
